# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

**Primary decision-makers:** Parents and guardians in Dublin, Pleasanton, and San Ramon who already invest in enrichment, compare tutoring options carefully, and worry about keeping pace with peers and competitive college admissions. Many come from communities where academic achievement is culturally emphasized; public-facing messaging stays inclusive and demographic-neutral even when referrals flow through community networks.

**Learners:** Middle and high school students in grades 6 through 12, with day-to-day focus on motivated high schoolers in rigorous coursework. Ideal fit: students who want to improve, not families seeking a fixed outsourced curriculum or purely remedial catch-up.

**Context:** Discovery and trust happen online; booking a free consult and understanding services before committing time and money.

## Product Purpose

The DTA website is the official public presence for Dublin Tutoring Association: explain who tutors are, what subjects and support they offer, convey the personalized and affordable alternative to large chains, and drive visitors to book a free initial consultation and contact the practice. Success means clear positioning, credible peer-level authority, and low-friction paths to schedule or reach out.

## Brand Personality

Warmly competent, specific rather than loud, peer-credible without arrogance. Voice: respectful of family time, honest about fit, confident in recent firsthand experience with AP coursework and college admissions. Three words: **credible, tailored, approachable.**

## Anti-references

Avoid the visual and tonal clichés of national tutoring franchises: high-pressure package selling, anonymous corporate polish, stock "success" imagery without substance, and cluttered pricing tables that obscure fit. Do not mimic generic SaaS landing patterns or AI-default layouts that feel interchangeable. Steer clear of worksheet-center or one-curriculum-fits-all cues that contradict the promise of true personalization.

## Design Principles

1. **Alignment:** The site should embody the same clarity and respect for attention that the practice promises in sessions.
2. **Credibility through specificity:** Lead with concrete offers, geography, and real differentiators rather than hollow superlatives.
3. **Human voice:** Recent graduates and small practice are strengths; show authenticity without hype.
4. **Fit over volume:** Messaging should help the right families recognize themselves, including gentle disqualifiers where appropriate.
5. **Inclusive surface, precise backend:** Public copy stays broadly welcoming; operational nuance lives in consultation and community channels.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Ship toward **WCAG 2.2 Level AA** for text contrast, focus visibility, semantic structure, and form labels. Respect **prefers-reduced-motion** for any motion beyond essential feedback. Write so multilingual households and mixed technical comfort levels can scan and act quickly; avoid idioms that exclude non-native English readers where simpler wording works.
